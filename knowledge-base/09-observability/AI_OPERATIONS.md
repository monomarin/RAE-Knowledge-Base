# Enterprise AI Operations Framework

> Official Enterprise AI Operations Framework for RAE Platform.

---

# Executive Summary

Artificial Intelligence is a core operational capability of RAE Platform.

The platform incorporates AI to assist engineers, automate operational analysis, predict failures, optimize infrastructure, coordinate incident response and continuously improve system reliability.

AI never replaces governance or engineering authority.

Human oversight remains mandatory for production-impacting decisions.

---

# Vision

Create a self-improving enterprise platform where Artificial Intelligence continuously assists operations while maintaining transparency, governance and human control.

---

# Strategic Objectives

- Improve operational efficiency.
- Predict failures before they occur.
- Reduce Mean Time To Detect (MTTD).
- Reduce Mean Time To Resolution (MTTR).
- Improve customer experience.
- Reduce operational cost.
- Increase platform resilience.
- Enable autonomous operations.

# AI Governance

Every AI decision shall include:

- Confidence Score
- Supporting Evidence
- Data Sources
- Reasoning Summary
- Timestamp
- Responsible Model
- Audit Record

Complete traceability is mandatory.

---

# AI Safety

AI shall never:

- Deploy production changes autonomously.
- Delete production data.
- Disable security controls.
- Ignore governance policies.
- Execute privileged actions without authorization.

---

# Human-in-the-Loop

Mandatory approval is required for:

- Production Deployments
- Infrastructure Changes
- Disaster Recovery
- Security Actions
- Executive Communications
- AI Model Replacement

---

# AI Model Management
