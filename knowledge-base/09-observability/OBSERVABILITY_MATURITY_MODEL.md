# Enterprise Observability Maturity Model

> Official Enterprise Observability Maturity Model for RAE Platform.

---

# Executive Summary

Observability is not a tool—it is an organizational capability.

The Enterprise Observability Maturity Model provides a structured method for evaluating how effectively RAE Platform measures, understands, predicts and continuously improves its technology and business operations.

The model covers technical excellence, operational maturity, executive governance, artificial intelligence, automation and continuous learning.

Its objective is to transform observability into a strategic competitive advantage.

---

# Vision

Achieve Autonomous Enterprise Observability through continuous evolution, measurable maturity and AI-assisted operational intelligence.

- Measure organizational maturity.

# Enterprise Maturity Levels

## Level 0 — Initial

Characteristics:

- Reactive Operations
- Limited Monitoring
- Manual Processes
- Minimal Documentation
- No Standardization

---

## Level 1 — Foundational

Characteristics:

- Centralized Monitoring
- Basic Logging
- Initial Dashboards
- Basic Alerts
- Operational Visibility

---

## Level 2 — Managed

Characteristics:

- Metrics Standardization
- Distributed Tracing
- Incident Processes
- Runbooks
- Defined SLOs

---

## Level 3 — Integrated

Characteristics:

- AI-Assisted Operations
- Predictive Analytics
- Enterprise Dashboards
- Automated Incident Response
- Knowledge Graph Integration

---

## Level 4 — Optimized

Characteristics:

- Autonomous Recommendations
- Continuous Reliability Engineering
- Enterprise Governance
- Advanced Automation
- Business Intelligence Integration

---

## Level 5 — Autonomous Enterprise

Characteristics:

- Self-Optimizing Operations
- Autonomous Intelligence
- AI Decision Support
- Continuous Learning
- Enterprise Digital Twin
- Predictive Governance
