# Enterprise Architecture Review Board (ARB)

> Official Enterprise Architecture Review Board for RAE Platform.

---

# Executive Summary

The Architecture Review Board (ARB) is the governing body responsible for preserving the architectural integrity of RAE Platform.

The board reviews significant technical proposals, approves architectural standards, evaluates risks and ensures that every major decision aligns with the enterprise vision, business strategy and architectural principles.

The ARB promotes consistency while enabling innovation through controlled governance.

---

# Vision

Provide transparent, collaborative and measurable architectural governance that enables sustainable long-term growth.

---

# Mission

The Architecture Review Board shall:

- Protect architectural consistency.
- Encourage innovation.
- Minimize technical debt.
- Reduce architectural risk.
- Improve decision quality.
- Preserve enterprise standards.
- Enable strategic scalability.
- Foster continuous improvement.

---

# Strategic Objectives

- Govern enterprise architecture.
- Review strategic initiatives.
- Approve architectural standards.
- Evaluate technology adoption.
- Resolve cross-domain conflicts.
- Maintain documentation quality.
- Promote engineering excellence.
- Guide enterprise evolution.

# Board Responsibilities

The ARB shall:

- Review strategic architecture.
- Approve ADRs.
- Review new technologies.
- Validate enterprise standards.
- Resolve architectural conflicts.
- Review major releases.
- Evaluate technical debt.
- Monitor architectural health.

---

# Decision Authority Matrix

The ARB approves:

- Enterprise Standards
- Cross-Domain Changes
- New Platforms
- Cloud Strategy
- Security Standards
- AI Strategy
- Canonical Model Changes
- Major Architecture Refactoring

Routine implementation decisions remain with engineering teams.

# Architecture Compliance Reviews

Periodic reviews verify:

- Architecture standards compliance
- Documentation quality
- API consistency
- Security alignment

# Exception Management

Architecture exceptions require:

- Written justification
- Risk assessment
- Mitigation plan
- Expiration date
- ARB approval

Exceptions are temporary by default.

---

# Meeting Cadence

Regular meetings:

- Weekly Technical Review
- Monthly Strategic Review
- Quarterly Enterprise Review
- Annual Architecture Assessment

Emergency meetings may be convened when required.

# AI-Assisted Architecture Reviews
