# Enterprise Engineering Scorecards

> Official Engineering Scorecards Framework for RAE Platform.

---

# Executive Summary

Engineering Scorecards provide a unified measurement system for evaluating the quality, maturity and operational excellence of engineering assets.

The framework transforms technical indicators into actionable intelligence for engineering teams, architects, SREs, executives and AI Agents.

Scorecards enable objective decision-making rather than subjective assessments.

---

# Vision

Create a continuously improving engineering organization driven by measurable excellence.

---

# Strategic Objectives

- Measure Engineering Quality
- Increase Platform Reliability
- Improve Architecture Compliance
- Reduce Technical Debt
- Enhance Developer Experience
- Improve Security
- Support AI-Assisted Governance
- Drive Continuous Improvement
