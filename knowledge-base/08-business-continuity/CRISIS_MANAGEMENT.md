# Enterprise Crisis Management

> Official Enterprise Crisis Management Framework for RAE Platform.

---

# Executive Summary

The Enterprise Crisis Management Framework provides the governance model for responding to major events that exceed normal operational response capabilities.

A crisis may affect technology, cybersecurity, AI services, business operations, legal compliance, reputation or human safety.

The framework enables coordinated executive decision-making, structured communications and rapid organizational recovery.

---

# Vision

Create an organization capable of responding decisively, transparently and effectively to any crisis while protecting customers, employees, partners and business continuity.

---

# Strategic Objectives

- Protect human safety.
- Preserve customer trust.
- Minimize operational disruption.
- Ensure executive coordination.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Protect organizational reputation.
- Improve crisis preparedness.
- Strengthen organizational resilience.

# Crisis Severity Levels

## Level 1

Operational disruption with limited business impact.

---

## Level 2

Significant business impact requiring executive oversight.

---

## Level 3

Enterprise-wide crisis requiring full Crisis Management Team activation.

When applicable, the organization shall notify:

- Regulatory Agencies

- Legal

# Customer Communications

Customer updates shall include:

- Service Status
- Impact Summary
- Expected Resolution
- Recommended Actions
- Contact Information

# AI Platform Crisis

AI-specific crises include:

- AI Provider Failure
- Model Compromise
- Prompt Injection Campaigns
- Hallucination at Scale
- Unauthorized AI Decisions

# Third-Party Crisis

Critical vendors shall:

- Notify RAE promptly
- Provide recovery estimates
- Share technical updates
- Participate in coordinated response

Vendor response performance shall be evaluated after the crisis.
