# Enterprise Business Continuity Management

> Official Enterprise Business Continuity Management (BCM) Standard for RAE Platform.

---

# Executive Summary

Business Continuity Management (BCM) establishes the enterprise framework that enables RAE Platform to continue delivering critical services during disruptive events.

The BCM program integrates organizational resilience, technology resilience, operational recovery and executive governance to minimize service interruption and business impact.

Business Continuity is an enterprise capability involving every department, not solely the IT organization.

---

# Vision

Create an AI-first SaaS platform capable of maintaining essential business operations under adverse conditions while minimizing operational, financial and reputational impact.

---

# Strategic Objectives

- Maintain critical business services.
- Protect customers.
- Improve organizational resilience.
- Reduce operational downtime.
- Strengthen executive preparedness.
- Protect AI services.
- Improve regulatory compliance.
- Enable continuous service delivery.

---

# Critical Business Services

Critical services include:

- SaaS Platform
- Authentication
- AI Platform
- Music Distribution
- Edge Synchronization
- Customer Portal
- API Platform
- Billing
- Monitoring
- Support Services

Each service shall have documented recovery objectives.

---

# Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

Each critical service shall define:

- Business Owner
- Criticality
- Maximum Tolerable Downtime (MTD)
- Recovery Time Objective (RTO)
- Recovery Point Objective (RPO)
- Financial Impact
- Operational Impact
- Customer Impact
- Regulatory Impact
- Dependencies

---

# Recovery Priorities

Priority 1

- Authentication

- AI Core
- Database Platform

Priority 2

- Monitoring
- Billing
- Messaging

# Crisis Management

A Crisis Management Team (CMT) shall include:

- Executive Sponsor
- Incident Commander
- Security Lead
- Infrastructure Lead
- AI Platform Lead
- Communications Lead
- Customer Success Lead
- Legal Representative

The CMT coordinates strategic decisions during major disruptions.
