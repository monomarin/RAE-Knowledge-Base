# Enterprise Knowledge Governance Framework

Official governance framework for enterprise knowledge.

---

# Executive Summary

Enterprise AI is only as trustworthy as the knowledge it consumes.

The Knowledge Governance Framework establishes ownership, stewardship, accountability, review processes and governance policies for all enterprise knowledge assets.

Knowledge is managed as a strategic corporate asset.

---

# Vision

Ensure every enterprise knowledge asset is owned, trusted, reviewed, governed and continuously improved.

---

# Strategic Objectives

- Knowledge Ownership
- Accountability
- Stewardship
- Lifecycle Governance
- Enterprise Compliance
- Policy Enforcement
- Business Alignment
- Continuous Improvement

---

# Governance Principles

Knowledge shall be:

- Owned
- Accountable
- Versioned
- Reviewed
- Classified
- Auditable
- Discoverable
- Governed

---

# Governance Organization

The governance model includes:

- Enterprise Knowledge Board
- Knowledge Owners
- Knowledge Stewards
- Domain Experts
- AI Governance Board
- Information Security
- Compliance Office

---

# Knowledge Ownership

Every knowledge asset defines:

- Business Owner
- Technical Owner
- Knowledge Steward
- Reviewer
- Approver
- Custodian

Ownership is mandatory.
