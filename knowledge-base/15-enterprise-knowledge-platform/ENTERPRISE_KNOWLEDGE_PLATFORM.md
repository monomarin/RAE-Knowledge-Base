# Enterprise Knowledge Platform

> Official Enterprise Knowledge Platform Architecture for RAE Platform.

---

# Executive Summary

The Enterprise Knowledge Platform is the cognitive foundation of RAE Platform.

It provides unified access to enterprise knowledge regardless of storage technology or knowledge representation.

The platform supports Knowledge Graphs, Vector Databases, SQL, Documents, APIs, Events and AI Memory through a common semantic layer.

---

# Vision

Create a unified enterprise knowledge ecosystem where every piece of organizational knowledge is discoverable, governed and reusable.
