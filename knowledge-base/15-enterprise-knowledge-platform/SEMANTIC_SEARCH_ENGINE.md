# Enterprise Semantic Search Engine

Official semantic retrieval engine for enterprise knowledge.

---

# Executive Summary

The Enterprise Semantic Search Engine enables intelligent knowledge discovery across structured, unstructured, semantic and experiential knowledge.

Instead of relying on exact keywords, the engine understands user intent, business terminology, relationships and organizational context.

Search results are ranked according to semantic relevance, governance policies, trust level and business context.

---

# Vision

Provide enterprise search that understands meaning rather than words.
