# Enterprise Knowledge Graph

> Official Enterprise Knowledge Graph Architecture for RAE Platform.

---

# Executive Summary

The Enterprise Knowledge Graph provides a unified semantic representation of the organization.

It enables reasoning across business, technical and operational domains while supporting explainability, impact analysis, semantic search and AI decision-making.

The graph continuously evolves as the enterprise changes.

---

# Vision

Represent the entire enterprise as a living semantic network that enables humans and AI to understand the organization through relationships rather than isolated data.
