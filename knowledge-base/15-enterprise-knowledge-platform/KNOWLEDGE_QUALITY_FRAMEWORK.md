# Enterprise Knowledge Quality Framework

> Official Enterprise Knowledge Quality Framework for RAE Platform.

---

# Executive Summary

Knowledge quality extends beyond traditional data quality.

A knowledge asset is valuable only when it is accurate, complete, connected, current, explainable and reusable.

The framework continuously evaluates knowledge throughout its lifecycle and produces measurable quality indicators.

---

# Vision

Create a self-improving knowledge ecosystem where trust continuously increases as knowledge evolves.

# Quality Principles

Knowledge shall be:

- Accurate
- Complete
- Consistent
- Current
- Connected
- Explainable
- Traceable
- Actionable

---

# Quality Dimensions

The framework evaluates:

## Accuracy

Is the knowledge correct?

---

## Completeness

Is all required information present?

---

## Freshness

Is the knowledge current?

---

## Consistency

Does it contradict other knowledge?

---

## Connectivity

Is it properly linked within the Knowledge Graph?

---

## Traceability

Can every statement be traced to its origin?

---

## Explainability

Can AI explain why this knowledge was used?

---

## Reusability

Can multiple domains consume this knowledge?
