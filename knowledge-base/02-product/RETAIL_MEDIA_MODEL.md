# Retail Media Model

> Official Retail Media architecture for RAE Platform.

---

# Executive Summary

Retail Media is the business capability that transforms RAE Platform into an intelligent advertising ecosystem.

The platform manages advertising inventory, campaign delivery, targeting, optimization, measurement and AI-driven recommendations across physical locations.

Retail Media is channel-agnostic and supports audio today while remaining prepared for future media channels.

---

# Design Principles

Every Retail Media capability must be:

- Measurable
- AI Ready
- Multi-Tenant
- Privacy Aware
- Auditable
- Revenue Oriented
- Extensible
- Vendor Independent

The Retail Media platform includes:
