# Audio Advertising Metrics

> Standard metrics for measuring the effectiveness of audio advertising campaigns in RAE Platform.

---

# Executive Summary

RAE Platform transforms audio advertising into measurable business intelligence.

Unlike traditional background music systems, every campaign is evaluated through operational, marketing and financial metrics.

The objective is to provide organizations with actionable insights rather than simple playback statistics.

---

# Measurement Philosophy

If a campaign cannot be measured, it cannot be optimized.

Every advertisement should generate data.

Every metric should support better business decisions.
