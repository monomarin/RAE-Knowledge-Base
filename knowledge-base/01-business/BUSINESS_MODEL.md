# Business Model

> Official business model for the RAE Platform ecosystem.

---

# Executive Summary

RAE Platform is designed as a recurring revenue platform.

Revenue is generated through software subscriptions, AI services, retail media capabilities, professional services and future marketplace integrations.

The architecture must support multiple monetization models simultaneously without requiring changes to the core platform.

---

# Business Philosophy

The objective is not simply to sell software.

The objective is to build a scalable ecosystem where customers, partners, developers and AI services generate continuous value.

Every new capability should create value for customers while strengthening recurring revenue.

---

# Revenue Streams

RAE Platform supports multiple revenue sources.

## SaaS Subscription

Recurring monthly or annual subscription.

Possible plans:

- Starter
- Professional
- Business
- Enterprise
- Enterprise Plus
- White Label

---

## AI Consumption

Customers pay according to AI usage.

Examples:

- AI Copywriter
- AI Campaign Assistant
- Mureka Music Generation
- Voice Generation
- Translation
- Campaign Optimization

The platform must remain provider-independent.

---

## Retail Media

Organizations may monetize their physical spaces.

Examples:

- Shopping malls selling advertising to tenants.
- Retail chains promoting internal campaigns.
- Franchise promotions.
- Third-party advertising.

RAE Platform manages scheduling, analytics and billing support.

---

## Premium Analytics

Advanced reporting modules.

Examples:

- Executive Dashboards
- Campaign Performance
- Audience Estimation
- ROI Analysis
- Operational Intelligence

---

## Professional Services

Optional consulting services.

Examples:

- Deployment
- Training
- Onboarding
- AI Configuration
- Integration Projects

---

## Marketplace (Future)

The platform will support a marketplace ecosystem.

Possible assets include:

- AI Agents
- Dashboard Templates
- Campaign Templates
- Audio Packs
- Music Styles
- Integrations
- Plugins
- Automation Flows

---

# Customer Plans

## Starter

Designed for small businesses.

Typical characteristics:

- Few locations.
- Basic scheduling.
- Limited AI usage.

---

## Professional

Designed for growing businesses.

Includes:

- Multiple locations.
- Campaign management.
- AI assistants.
- Analytics.

---

## Business

Designed for regional organizations.

Includes:

- Multi-region support.
- Advanced automation.
- Multiple users.
- Extended reporting.

---

## Enterprise

Designed for large organizations.

Includes:

- Unlimited hierarchy.
- Advanced permissions.
- API access.
- Integrations.
- White-label options.
- Enterprise support.

---

## White Label

Partners can operate RAE Platform under their own brand.

Includes:

- Custom branding.
- Custom domains.
- Independent tenants.
- Partner administration.

---

# Pricing Dimensions

Pricing should be flexible.

Possible dimensions include:

- Number of locations.
- Number of audio zones.
- Number of users.
- Number of devices.
- AI consumption.
- Generated content.
- Storage.
- API usage.
- Monthly audience.
- Advertising volume.

No single pricing metric should limit future business models.

---

# Marketplace Economy

Future marketplace participants:

- Developers
- AI Providers
- Marketing Agencies
- Music Producers
- Integrators
- Technology Partners

Revenue sharing policies will be defined separately.

---

# Partner Ecosystem

Future partners may include:

- Audio Hardware Vendors
- Camera Providers
- POS Integrators
- ERP Vendors
- CRM Platforms
- AI Providers
- Cloud Providers

The platform must remain vendor-neutral.

---

# Expansion Strategy

The business model is designed for international expansion.

Target markets include:

- Latin America
- North America
- Europe
- Middle East
- Asia-Pacific

Localization and multilingual support are core platform capabilities.

---

# Success Indicators

The business model is successful when:

- Recurring revenue grows predictably.
- Customer retention remains high.
- AI usage increases customer value.
- Retail Media generates new income streams.
- Marketplace participation expands the ecosystem.
