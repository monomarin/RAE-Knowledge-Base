# Retail Media

> Official Retail Media strategy for RAE Platform.

---

# Executive Summary

Retail Media is one of the strategic pillars of RAE Platform.

Instead of viewing audio as background music, the platform treats every speaker, store, shopping mall and commercial space as a measurable communication channel capable of generating business value.

Retail Media becomes an integrated capability rather than an isolated product.

---

# Vision

Every physical location has media inventory.

Every speaker represents advertising inventory.

Every promotional campaign represents measurable business value.

RAE Platform transforms physical locations into intelligent retail media networks.

---

# Retail Media Hub

The Retail Media Hub is responsible for managing:

- Audio advertising
- Promotional campaigns
- Sponsored announcements
- Brand campaigns
- Internal communications
- Seasonal promotions
- National campaigns
- Regional campaigns
- Local campaigns

Everything from a single platform.
