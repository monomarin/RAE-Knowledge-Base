# ROI Model

> Official Return on Investment framework for RAE Platform.

---

# Executive Summary

The ROI Model demonstrates the measurable business value created by RAE Platform.

The objective is to quantify financial, operational and strategic benefits generated through intelligent audio management, AI automation and Retail Media capabilities.

Every investment should be supported by measurable outcomes.

---

# ROI Philosophy

Organizations invest in outcomes, not software.

RAE Platform measures success through continuous business improvement.

The ROI model provides objective evidence of platform value.
