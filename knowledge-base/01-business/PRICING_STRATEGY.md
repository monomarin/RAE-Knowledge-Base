# Pricing Strategy

> Official pricing strategy for the RAE Platform ecosystem.

---

# Executive Summary

Pricing is one of the strategic pillars of RAE Platform.

The objective is not to sell software licenses, but to deliver measurable business value through a scalable, AI-First and modular pricing model.

The pricing strategy must remain flexible enough to support different industries, customer sizes and future capabilities.

---

# Pricing Principles

The pricing model shall:

- Scale with customer growth.
- Be easy to understand.
- Avoid hidden costs.
- Encourage platform adoption.
- Support international markets.
- Remain independent of technology providers.

---

# Pricing Philosophy

Customers should pay according to the value they receive.

Growth in platform usage should naturally align with increased business value.

---

# Pricing Dimensions

The platform supports multiple pricing variables.

Possible dimensions include:

- Number of tenants
- Number of locations
- Number of stores
- Number of audio zones
- Number of devices
- Number of users
- Number of campaigns
- AI consumption
- Generated music
- Voice generation
- API requests
- Storage usage
- Analytics retention
- Marketplace transactions

Future pricing dimensions can be introduced without affecting the platform architecture.

# Subscription Plans

## Starter

Target:

Small businesses.

Characteristics:

- Limited locations.
- Basic scheduling.
- Basic analytics.

---

## Professional

Target:

Growing businesses.

Characteristics:

- Multi-location support.
- AI assistants.
- Campaign automation.
- Advanced scheduling.

---

## Business

Target:

Regional organizations.

Characteristics:

- Multiple regions.
- Team collaboration.
- Advanced analytics.
- Retail Media.

---

## Enterprise

Target:

Large organizations.

Characteristics:

- Unlimited hierarchy.
- Advanced permissions.
- API access.
- SSO.
- SLA.
- White-label options.

---

## Enterprise Plus

Target:

Global enterprises.

Characteristics:

- Dedicated infrastructure.
- Premium support.
- Enterprise integrations.
- Advanced compliance.
- Custom AI capabilities.

Artificial Intelligence services are usage-based.

Examples:

- Music Generation
- Translation
