# Customer Segments

> Definition of every customer segment supported by RAE Platform.

---

# Executive Summary

RAE Platform is designed as a horizontal platform.

It is not limited to supermarkets.

Any organization operating one or more physical locations where ambient audio, promotional communication or customer engagement creates business value can use the platform.

Customer segmentation allows RAE Platform to adapt modules, dashboards, analytics, AI assistants and commercial strategies to different industries while maintaining a single technology platform.

---

# Segmentation Strategy

Customers are segmented using multiple dimensions.

- Industry
- Organization Size
- Number of Locations
- Operational Complexity
- Audio Maturity
- Digital Transformation Level

This enables flexible product packaging and pricing.

# Multi-Tenant Philosophy

Every customer is isolated as an independent tenant.

Each tenant may contain:

- Regions
- Cities
- Branches
- Stores
- Buildings
- Floors
- Audio Zones

The architecture must support unlimited hierarchy depth.
