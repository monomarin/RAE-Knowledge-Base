# Knowledge Model

> Official knowledge architecture for RAE Platform.

---

# Executive Summary

The Knowledge Model defines how knowledge is created, structured, validated, stored, related, retrieved and evolved throughout the entire RAE Platform ecosystem.

Knowledge is treated as a strategic enterprise asset shared by humans and AI agents.

---

# Vision

Build a living organizational memory capable of supporting autonomous agents, business users and future AI capabilities.

---

# Design Principles

Every knowledge asset must be:

- Structured
- Searchable
- Explainable
- Versioned
- Traceable
- AI Ready
- Reusable
- Governed
- Vendor Independent

# Quality Metrics

Knowledge quality is measured by:

- Completeness
- Accuracy
- Freshness
- Reusability
- Traceability
- AI Retrieval Success

---

# Success Criteria

The Knowledge Model is successful when:

- Knowledge is reusable.
- AI retrieves relevant information consistently.
- Relationships are preserved.
- Organizational learning accumulates over time.
- Knowledge evolves without losing traceability.
