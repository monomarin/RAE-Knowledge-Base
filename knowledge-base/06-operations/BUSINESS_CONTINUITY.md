# Business Continuity

> Official Business Continuity Management Standard for RAE Platform.

---

# Executive Summary

Business Continuity Management (BCM) establishes the strategic framework that enables RAE Platform to continue delivering critical business services during disruptive events.

Unlike Disaster Recovery, which focuses on restoring technology, Business Continuity ensures the continuity of business operations, decision-making, customer support, communications and organizational resilience.

---

# Vision

Maintain uninterrupted delivery of critical business services through proactive planning, operational resilience and continuous preparedness.

---

# Objectives

- Protect critical business functions.
- Ensure customer service continuity.
- Reduce business disruption.
- Protect organizational reputation.
- Maintain regulatory compliance.
- Coordinate crisis response.
- Support Disaster Recovery.
- Continuously improve organizational resilience.

---
