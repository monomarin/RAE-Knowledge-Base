# Operations Model

> Official operating model for RAE Platform.

---

# Executive Summary

This document defines the operational model used to manage, monitor, maintain and evolve RAE Platform in production environments.

Operations encompass technology, people, automation and governance to ensure platform reliability and continuous availability.

---

# Vision

Operate the platform as a resilient, observable and continuously improving cloud-native service.

---

# Operations Principles

Platform operations must be:

- Reliable
- Automated
- Observable
- Secure
- Predictable
- Scalable
- Measurable
- Cost Efficient

---

# Success Criteria

The operational model is successful when:

- Platform availability remains high.
- Recovery times decrease.
- Customer impact is minimized.
- Operational knowledge grows continuously.
- Automation replaces repetitive work.
