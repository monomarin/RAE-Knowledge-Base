# Enterprise Multi-Agent Collaboration Framework

> Official Enterprise Multi-Agent Collaboration Framework for RAE Platform.

---

# Executive Summary

Modern enterprise AI systems require collaboration among multiple specialized agents.

The framework standardizes:

- Communication
- Delegation
- Negotiation
- Consensus
- Knowledge Sharing
- Conflict Resolution
- Human Collaboration
- Collective Decision Making

Every collaboration follows enterprise governance.

---

# Vision

Create an enterprise digital workforce where AI Agents operate as coordinated teams rather than isolated assistants.

---

# Strategic Objectives

- Distributed Intelligence
- Team-Based AI
- Autonomous Coordination
- Shared Knowledge
- Enterprise Governance
- Human Collaboration
- Continuous Learning
- Business Outcome Optimization

# Collaboration Principles

Every collaboration shall be:

- Goal Driven
- Role Based
- Transparent
- Explainable
- Secure
- Observable
- Governed
- Auditable

# Agent Roles

Supported roles include:

- Coordinator
- Planner
- Specialist
- Reviewer
- Validator
- Researcher
- Executor
- Auditor
- Observer
- Human Supervisor

Roles are dynamic and may change during execution.

# Knowledge Sharing

Agents may exchange:

- Facts
- Plans
- Results
- Evidence
- Risks
- Context
- Memory References

Knowledge sharing follows tenant isolation rules.

---

# Consensus Mechanisms

Supported mechanisms:

- Majority Vote
- Weighted Vote
- Expert Review
- Confidence Scoring
- Human Approval

Consensus strategy depends on business criticality.

---

# Conflict Resolution

Conflicts may arise from:

- Divergent Recommendations
- Resource Competition
- Policy Violations
- Knowledge Inconsistencies

Resolution methods include:

- Automatic Arbitration
- Confidence Ranking
- Human Escalation
- Policy Enforcement

---

# Human Collaboration

Humans participate as:

- Supervisors
- Reviewers
- Decision Makers
- Domain Experts

Human intervention is configurable by policy.
