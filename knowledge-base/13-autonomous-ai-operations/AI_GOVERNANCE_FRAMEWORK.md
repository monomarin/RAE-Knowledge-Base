# Enterprise AI Governance Framework

Official governance framework for all AI capabilities within RAE Platform.

---

# Executive Summary

The Enterprise AI Governance Framework defines the principles, organizational responsibilities, controls and decision-making processes that ensure artificial intelligence is developed and operated responsibly.

It aligns AI innovation with enterprise strategy while protecting customers, employees and business operations.

---

# Vision

Create a trustworthy AI ecosystem where every intelligent capability is transparent, secure, accountable and aligned with organizational values.

---

# Strategic Objectives

- Responsible AI
- Enterprise Trust
- Risk Management
- Regulatory Compliance
- Human Oversight
- Operational Excellence
- Continuous Governance
- Sustainable Innovation

Every AI capability shall be:

# Governance Organization

The governance structure includes:

- Enterprise AI Governance Board
- AI Platform Team
- Enterprise Architecture
- Information Security
- Legal & Compliance
- Risk Management
- Business Owners
- Human Supervisors

Each role has clearly defined responsibilities.

# Decision Rights

Governance decisions are classified as:

- Strategic
- Tactical
- Operational
- Emergency

Approval authority depends on decision criticality.

# Risk Management

AI risks include:

- Hallucinations
- Bias
- Privacy Violations
- Prompt Injection
- Model Drift
- Vendor Dependency
- Regulatory Non-Compliance
- Unsafe Autonomous Actions

Each risk has defined mitigation controls.

---

# Human Oversight

Human review is mandatory for:

- Financial Decisions
- Legal Commitments
- Safety-Critical Operations
- Compliance Exceptions
- High-Risk Recommendations

Oversight policies are configurable by business domain.
