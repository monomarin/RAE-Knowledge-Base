# Enterprise AI Evaluation Framework

Official quality framework for all AI capabilities within RAE Platform.

---

# Executive Summary

The AI Evaluation Framework continuously measures the performance of enterprise AI systems across technical, operational and business dimensions.

Evaluation is not limited to language model accuracy; it encompasses prompts, memory, retrieval, orchestration, tool usage and business outcomes.

Results drive automated improvements and governance decisions.

---

# Vision

Create a continuously learning AI ecosystem where every interaction contributes to measurable quality improvements.
