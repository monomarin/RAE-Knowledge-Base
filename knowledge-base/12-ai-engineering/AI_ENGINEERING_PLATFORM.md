# Enterprise AI Engineering Platform

> Official AI Engineering Platform for RAE Platform.

---

# Executive Summary

The AI Engineering Platform (AIEP) is the enterprise foundation for building, operating and governing all Artificial Intelligence capabilities across RAE Platform.

Rather than treating AI as an isolated component, the platform integrates AI into every architectural layer, enabling standardized AI services, reusable components and enterprise-wide governance.

It supports multiple LLM providers, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), long-term memory, AI evaluation pipelines and intelligent orchestration.

---

# Vision

Create a secure, scalable and vendor-agnostic AI platform that empowers every product, service and engineering team to leverage artificial intelligence responsibly and efficiently.

# AI Gateway

The AI Gateway provides a unified interface for every AI provider.

Responsibilities include:

- Authentication
- Authorization
- Rate Limiting
- Request Routing
- Cost Tracking
- Usage Analytics
- Policy Enforcement

Applications never communicate directly with external AI providers.

---

# Model Router

The Model Router selects the most appropriate model based on:

- Cost
- Latency
- Accuracy
- Availability
- Context Size
- Regulatory Constraints
- Business Policies

Routing decisions are dynamic and policy-driven.
