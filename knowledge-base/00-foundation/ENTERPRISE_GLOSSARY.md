# Enterprise Knowledge Dictionary

Official terminology reference for the entire RAE Platform.

---

# Executive Summary

The Enterprise Knowledge Dictionary is the authoritative source for every technical, architectural, operational and business term used across RAE Platform.

Instead of each document defining concepts independently, terminology is centralized, versioned and governed.

This ensures semantic consistency across documentation, AI Agents, APIs and business processes.

---

# Vision

Provide a single semantic language understood by humans, AI Agents and software systems.

---

# Dictionary Principles

Every term shall be:
