# Enterprise Risk Management

> Official Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) Standard for RAE Platform.

---

# Executive Summary

Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) provides a unified governance framework for identifying, evaluating, treating and continuously monitoring risks that may affect RAE Platform.

Risk is evaluated from technical, operational, financial, legal, strategic and AI governance perspectives.

ERM is integrated into every stage of architecture, software development, cloud operations, AI lifecycle and executive planning.

---

# Vision

Create an AI-first enterprise where every strategic and operational decision is supported by continuous, measurable and intelligent risk management.

---

# Strategic Objectives

- Protect enterprise value.
- Improve executive decision making.
- Reduce operational uncertainty.
- Strengthen cyber resilience.
- Govern AI responsibly.
- Improve regulatory readiness.
- Optimize investments.
- Build organizational resilience.
