# Enterprise Privacy Policy

> Official Enterprise Privacy Governance Standard for RAE Platform.

---

# Executive Summary

Privacy Governance establishes the principles, controls and operational processes that ensure lawful, transparent and ethical processing of personal information across the RAE Platform.

Every product, AI service, API, Edge Node and operational workflow must implement Privacy by Design and Privacy by Default.

Privacy is treated as an enterprise capability rather than solely a legal requirement.

---

# Vision

Build one of the world's most privacy-respecting AI platforms by embedding privacy into architecture, software engineering, AI systems and operational governance.

---

# Strategic Objectives

- Protect personal information.
- Respect customer privacy.
- Comply with global privacy regulations.
- Enable Privacy by Design.
- Govern AI data usage.
- Reduce privacy risks.
- Increase transparency.
- Strengthen customer trust.

---

Applies to:

- Customers

- Voice Assistants
- APIs

- Integrations

# Privacy Principles

Every privacy decision follows:

- Lawfulness
- Fairness
- Transparency
- Purpose Limitation
- Data Minimization
- Accuracy
- Storage Limitation
- Integrity
- Confidentiality
- Accountability

---

# Privacy by Design

Privacy shall be incorporated into:

- System Architecture
- Product Design
- AI Agents
- APIs
- Databases
- Infrastructure
- Analytics
- Development Lifecycle

Privacy reviews occur before implementation.

---

# Privacy by Default

Default settings shall:

- Collect minimum data.
- Limit retention.
- Restrict access.
- Disable unnecessary tracking.
- Protect customer identities.
- Minimize AI memory.

---

# Personal Data Categories

Examples include:

- Name
- Email
- Phone Number
- IP Address
- Device Identifier
- Voice Recordings
- Authentication Data
- Payment Metadata
- Customer Preferences
- AI Conversation Metadata

Sensitive personal data receives additional protections.

---

# Data Subject Rights

The platform supports:

- Right of Access
- Right to Rectification
- Right to Erasure
- Right to Restrict Processing
- Right to Data Portability
- Right to Object
- Right to Withdraw Consent
- Right to Human Review of Automated Decisions

Requests shall be processed within applicable legal timeframes.

---

# Consent Management

Consent must be:

- Explicit where required.
- Freely Given.
- Granular.
- Revocable.
- Auditable.
- Easily Accessible.

Consent records shall be retained as evidence.

---

# AI Privacy Governance

AI systems shall:

- Minimize personal information.
- Avoid unnecessary retention.
- Support prompt redaction.
- Protect conversation history.
- Restrict AI memory.
- Prevent cross-tenant exposure.
- Support customer deletion requests.

AI models shall not use customer data for training unless explicitly authorized.

---

# Cross-Border Data Transfers

International transfers require:

- Legal Basis
- Approved Safeguards
- Encryption
- Risk Assessment
- Contractual Controls
- Regulatory Compliance

Regional data residency must be respected where required.

---

# Privacy Impact Assessments (PIA)

PIAs are required for:

- New Products
- AI Features
- Large-Scale Processing
- Sensitive Data
- Cross-Border Transfers
- New Integrations

---

# Privacy Incident Management

Privacy incidents include:

- Unauthorized Disclosure
- Personal Data Leakage
- AI Information Exposure
- Misconfigured Access
- Accidental Sharing
- Cross-Tenant Exposure

Privacy incidents follow the Incident Management process with additional regulatory reporting requirements.

---

# AI Agent Responsibilities

AI Agents may assist with:

- Privacy Classification
- Consent Verification
- Data Minimization Checks
- Privacy Impact Assessment Drafts
- Compliance Reporting
- Deletion Verification

AI Agents shall never override privacy decisions or legal obligations.
