# Enterprise Compliance Framework

> Official Enterprise Compliance Framework for RAE Platform.

---

# Executive Summary

The Enterprise Compliance Framework provides a centralized governance model that aligns cybersecurity, privacy, AI governance and operational controls with internationally recognized standards.

The framework enables continuous compliance monitoring, automated evidence collection and unified reporting across the organization.

Compliance is integrated into architecture, development, operations and AI lifecycle management.

---

# Vision

Build an AI-native SaaS platform where compliance is continuously verified, automatically measured and inherently embedded into every business process.

---

- Centralize compliance governance.
- Reduce audit complexity.
- Automate evidence collection.
- Improve regulatory readiness.
- Govern AI responsibly.

# Unified Control Framework

Controls are mapped once and reused across multiple standards.

Example:

```text
Multi-Factor Authentication

↓

ISO 27001
SOC 2
NIST CSF
CIS Controls
Zero Trust
```

A single implementation satisfies multiple compliance obligations.
